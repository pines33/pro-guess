How to play
  • Vs a friend: you each pick a secret pro, then take turns guessing the other one's player. First to guess right wins.
  • Solo: the server picks a random pro – find them in as few guesses as possible.
  • Green = same value. Yellow = close: age within 2 years, titles within 1, or one of you is a Flex player.
  • ↑ / ↓ = the secret player's age or title count is higher / lower.
  • Titles = international trophies (Masters & Champions).
